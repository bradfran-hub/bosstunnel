ONE LAYER. THREE SIMPLE STEPS.

Less friction. More connection.

Different sources, a shared language.
From your library to the apps you build.

01

Ingest

Connect supported media inputs like Xtream, Emby, Plex, Jellyfin, Stremio, and M3U / M3U8.

02

Normalize

Bring multiple source types into one consistent media layer, with shared identities and source-aware capabilities.

03

Deliver

Output Xtream, Manifest or BOSS endpoints for downstream apps and services. Resolve playback when it is requested.

Capabilities depend on each source and player. BOSS preserves supported functionality; it does not bypass access controls or transcode media.
